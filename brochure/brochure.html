About usWho we are
FreshNet Farms Pvt Ltd

A food-crop value chain enterprise building a global marketplace for India’s small-holding farmers.

We work closely with small-holding farmers, aggregators, harvesters and processors to add significant value at every node of the supply chain — for stakeholders in India and for buyers around the world.

Being very close to the farms lets us make a real impact on productivity and post-harvest processes, raising farmers’ income on a per-unit basis.

Along the chain we generate employment for landless workers and help them become skilled workers — improving capability and standard of living.

Today our focus is coconut and its value-added products, spices, coffee beans, pulses and major fruits.
